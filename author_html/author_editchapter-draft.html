The throne room had stood empty for a hundred years. Dust gathered on the obsidian seat like snow that would never melt, and the great banners of House Aldren — a silver stag on a field of deep blue — hung motionless in the still air.

No one had dared to sit there since the last king fell. No one had dared to claim it, either — not when the prophecy still whispered through every tavern from the coast to the highlands: when the stag returns, the kingdom will burn before it bows.

And yet, on the coldest morning of the year, a stranger walked through the broken gates of the capital, dressed in travelling clothes and carrying nothing but a sword that had seen better days. She did not stop at the inn. She did not stop at the market. She walked straight to the castle, past the guards who did not recognise her, and stopped before the empty throne.
