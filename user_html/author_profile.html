Biography

Wordsmith, social observer, and chronicler of modern life. After getting a harsh taste of the real world at age 12—working grueling shifts at a local packaging warehouse while my dad was locked up over mounting credit debt—I quickly learned that the best stories come from the rawest experiences. That trial by fire sparked my passion for giving a voice to the overlooked, the underdogs, and the dreamers.

Now, after going back to finish my education and cutting my teeth with freelance content writing and journalism, I’m fully focused on writing long-form fiction. My mission is simple: craft unforgettable characters and hold up a mirror to today's society through gripping, deeply human narrative.
