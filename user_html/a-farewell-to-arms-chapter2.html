Chapter 2

The Front

The next year there were many victories. The mountain that was beyond the valley and the hillside where the chestnut forest grew was captured and there were victories beyond the plain on the plateau to the south and we crossed the river in August and lived in a house in Gorizia that had a fountain and many thick shady trees in a walled garden and a wisteria vine purple on the side of the house. Now the fighting was in the next mountains beyond and was not a mile away. The town was very nice and our house was very fine. The river ran behind us and the town had been captured very handsomely but the mountains beyond it could not be taken and I was very glad the Austrians seemed to want to come back to the town some time, if the war should end, because they did not bombard it to destroy it but only a little.

There were many girls in the town and the officers were very gallant and there was much gaiety. The houses were all fine and there were two brothels for the officers and one for the men. The girls were very pretty and the wine was very good.

I was in the town of Gorizia and the fighting was in the mountains. The weather was fine and the autumn was coming. The leaves were turning and the vineyards were beautiful. The town was full of soldiers and there was much noise and confusion.

I went to the front to see the fighting. It was very quiet and there was a great deal of rain. The trenches were full of water and the men were wet and cold. I was glad to get back to the town.
