Our Story

We believe good stories shouldn't be locked away.

NovelNest started with a simple frustration: the rising cost of reading. We wanted to build a place where readers could explore thousands of novels for free, without compromising the livelihood of the writers who create them.

The Problem with Paywalls

In recent years, the internet became a place where you had to subscribe to five different platforms just to read a few chapters. We hated it. We also knew that authors deserved to be paid for their time and creativity.

So, we asked ourselves: what if we could make the reading experience completely free for the user, while still generating fair revenue for the author?

How NovelNest Works

We introduced a simple, ad-supported model. When you read a chapter, you'll occasionally see a brief advertisement. The revenue from those ads goes directly back to the authors. This means readers never have to pay a dime, and writers get paid based on how much their stories are actually being read.

It’s a quiet, sustainable ecosystem. No aggressive subscription pop-ups. No locked chapters. Just you, the reader, and the story.

Our Principles

  • Reading is free. Always. No hidden fees or premium tiers.
  • Authors are paid. Ad revenue is shared transparently with the writers.
  • Quality over noise. We manually review submissions to ensure a great reading experience.
