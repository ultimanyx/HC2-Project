Chapter 2

Black Dog Appears and Disappears

It was not long before the blind man had left us, that we began to be sensible of a change in the weather. The wind was shifting and blowing in gusts, and the rain was falling heavily. The captain was in his usual place, in the corner by the fire, drinking his rum and water, and looking as black as thunder.

"Captain," says my father, "if you don't mind, I'd be glad if you'd keep your voice a bit lower. My son is sick, and the noise disturbs him."

The captain turned his head and looked at my father with a scowl. "I'll talk as loud as I please," says he. "And if you don't like it, you can lump it."

My father was a timid man, and he said no more. But the captain's behaviour grew worse and worse. He would sit for hours in the corner, drinking and swearing, and sometimes he would take out a short, heavy cutlass and lay it on the table before him. He never went out, and he never let anyone else go out, unless they were going to fetch him more rum.

One day, a man came to the inn door. He was a pale, tallowy creature, wanting two fingers of the left hand, and though he wore a cutlass, he did not look much like a fighter. I had always my eye open for seafaring men, with one leg or two, and I remember this one puzzled me. He was not sailorly, and yet he had a smack of the sea about him too.

"Is this here table for my mate Bill?" he asked, with a kind of a leer.

I told him I did not know his mate Bill, and this was for a person who stayed in our house whom we called the captain.

"Well," said he, "my mate Bill would be called the captain, as like as not. He has a cut on one cheek, and a mighty pretty one it is. He's a tall man, and he has a sabre cut across his cheek. That's my mate Bill. And now, is he here?"

I told him he was out walking.

"Which way?" he asked.

I pointed down the road. He thanked me, and went off. But he had not gone far before he turned back and came up to me again.

"Look here," says he, "you tell my mate Bill that Black Dog has been here. Do you see? Black Dog. And tell him I'll be back."
