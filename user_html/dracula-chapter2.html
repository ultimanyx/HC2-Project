Chapter 2

Jonathan Harker's Journal (Continued)

But I cannot forget the strange events of the night. I must try to be calm, and to remember that I am a guest here, and that the Count has been most kind to me.7 May. — It is early morning, and I am writing this in my diary. The sun is shining brightly through the windows, and the castle looks less gloomy than it did last night.

I had a strange dream last night. I dreamt that I was in a dark place, and that I could hear the sound of water dripping. Then I saw a woman, very beautiful, with long dark hair. But just as she was about to touch me, the Count appeared, and drove her away. I woke up in a cold sweat, and found that I was in my own bed. She came towards me, and I felt a terrible fear.

This morning I explored the castle a little. It is a vast place, full of dark corridors and empty rooms. I found a library, filled with books in many languages. I spent some time there, looking at the books, and trying to understand the history of this place. But I could not find any book that told me about the Count himself.

I also found a room that was locked. I tried the door, but it would not open. I asked the Count about it later, and he said that it was a room that had not been opened for many years, and that it was not safe to enter. I did not press him further, but I could not help wondering what was inside.

The Count has been very attentive to me. He has asked me many questions about England, and about the people there. He seems to be planning to move there, and he wants to know everything about it. I have told him what I know, but I cannot help feeling that there is something strange about him. His eyes are very bright, and his teeth are very white. And he never seems to eat or drink anything.
