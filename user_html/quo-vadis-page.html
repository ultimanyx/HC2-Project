Synopsis

Quo Vadis: A Narrative of the Time of Nero is a historical novel written by Henryk Sienkiewicz. The novel is set in ancient Rome during the reign of Emperor Nero (c. 64–68 AD). It follows the love story of a young Christian woman, Lycia, and a Roman patrician, Marcus Vinicius. The narrative intertwines their romance with the brutal persecution of Christians by Nero, culminating in the Great Fire of Rome and the subsequent martyrdoms in the arena.
